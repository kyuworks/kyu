# Short active title

**Status:** proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by [title](YYYYMMDD-slug.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Parent:** [#N](https://github.com/Camba-nz/qtaxis/issues/N)
**This is not** the neighbouring decisions this record does not make.

One or two sentences: the single choice this record makes, and the next
change it stops someone from shipping.

---

## Context

The problem as it stands today. The forces: constraints, costs, latency,
compliance, team size, what the code already does. Facts only — cite files,
tables, migrations, issues. Do not name the chosen option here.

Write for someone who joins in three years and was in none of the meetings.

---

## Options considered

**A. Name of the option.** Two or three sentences. What it buys, what it
costs, why it lost.

**B. Name of the option.** Same.

**C. Name of the chosen option.** Same, ending in why it won.

Listing the losers is what proves the decision was not arbitrary, and it
stops the next person redoing the research.

---

## Decision

Active, present tense. "We use…", "We store…", "We do not add…".
Not "we decided to" and not "we should probably".

1. **The rule.** One or two lines of detail.
2. **The next rule.** One or two lines of detail.

---

## Consequences

**Positive**

- What this unblocks. What gets easier or cheaper.

**Negative**

- What gets harder. The maintenance burden or debt we accept on purpose.
- If this list is empty, the trade-offs are not understood yet.

---

## Do not

- The specific changes that contradict this decision, so review can point here.

---

## Reopen when

- The fact that would make this decision wrong. Omit the section if there
  isn't one.
